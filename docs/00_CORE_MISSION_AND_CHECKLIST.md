## 1. Owner Mission

Build a high-ceiling, mathematically coherent and scalable PICF architecture on
a strong open VLA. Preserve the useful theoretical and experimental evidence
from the historical project without carrying forward its accidental modules,
dataset-specific rules or leakage paths.

The required scientific core is:

1. a bounded, set-valued collection of anchors represents uncertain physical
   objects or meaningful physical parts across time; permutation equivariance
   and query specialization are measured rather than assumed;
2. same-object evidence from vision, video/world prediction, 3D, touch and
   proprioception is related in one shared latent information graph while
   modality-private information remains available;
3. dense native sensor tokens remain action-visible; persistent rows structure
   and address evidence rather than replacing it;
4. the prior predicts from previous posterior, executed action and elapsed
   time; current evidence produces a posterior; paired prior/posterior rows can
   change action;
5. occlusion removes a measurement, not necessarily the object, and uncertainty
   must reflect the distinction;
6. missing modalities contribute no factor, allowing one model and one data
   interface to scale across heterogeneous public datasets and embodiments;
7. masks, boxes, object IDs, future frames and simulator state are optional
   loss-side supervision only and never deploy inputs;
8. architecture changes must be principled and causally testable, not a chain
   of patches that only improves one CALVIN curve.

## 3. Stop Conditions

Stop and report that the direction should change if any of the following is
established:

- the task-independent cross-time object posterior is causally useless to
  action under a matched strong-host comparison;
- benefits require masks, boxes, simulator IDs, future information, fixed screen
  regions, task lookup or another deploy-time sidecar;
- a simpler matched memory baseline dominates PICF on action, recovery,
  generalization and systems cost;
- the design cannot preserve all legal dense evidence while maintaining one
  bounded posterior and one action path;
- heterogeneous modalities require dataset-specific posterior mathematics
  rather than typed interfaces and missing-factor semantics;
- the production rewrite can only retain the old fixed-fusion decisions under
  new names.

Current verdict: **continue as a falsifiable hypothesis, not as a demonstrated
next-generation model.**

## 4. Normative Architecture

ADR-74 is the owner-approved theory-freeze authority; ADR-75 is its completed
ten-pass adversarial audit. ADR-83 is the normative correction to the temporal
estimand and visible-support estimator discovered by ADR-81/82. Together they
amend ADR-71. Their non-negotiable boundary is:

- one deeply shared LingBot physical information graph;
- typed input projections and one typed linear posterior readout are allowed;
- independent physical identity, lifecycle or modality-authority modules are
  forbidden; presence/mask/relevance may exist only as readouts that never
  write or gate state;
- physical support is task-language-free;
- object/context/null assignment is soft and uncertainty-preserving;
- task-independent physical support may be exposed by typed readouts, but
  ADR-116 rejects one final language state and one shared normalized cosine
  space as the sole task-to-object interface;
- complete prompt and selected dense modality tokens must survive through at
  least one content-dependent interaction inside the shared LingBot host;
  learned compression after interaction must be claim-matched and causally
  validated;
- host-width posterior rows are the only cross-step learned state; lifecycle,
  geometry, covariance, age and innovation are not separate recurrent state;
- prior and posterior are jointly emitted by one layerwise triangular LingBot
  graph; they share weights and differ by causal visibility rather than two
  fictitious serial full passes;
- action reads dense evidence and paired prior/posterior rows;
- no fixed reliability table, hand-written confidence decay, analytic
  post-host fusion, teacher/student runtime or hidden second memory.

The former single shared relation-output rule is terminally rejected by
ADR-116. ADR-117 was the owner-authorized bounded replacement: one ephemeral
paired MATCH token per posterior row performs complete-prompt/sensor/row
interaction inside the same LingBot host, followed only by a shared scalar
readout. ADR-118 records its local implementation closure, and ADR-119 records
the terminal released-weight `FAIL`. No action adoption or long training is
authorized.

The current fixed-fusion implementation is comparison arm D, not production.
The exact rejected identifiers and the only approved replacement writer are
listed in ADR-71 Sections 12-14.
